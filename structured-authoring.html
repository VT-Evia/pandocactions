Structuring Your Requests

A well-structured request is easier for an AI to act on correctly, and easier for you to review afterward. A few habits go a long way:

  • State the goal first. Lead with what you want, not just background details.
  • Give relevant context. Mention constraints, prior attempts, or examples that shape a good answer.
  • Break down complex asks. Large, multi-part requests are easier to get right one step at a time.
  • Say what “done” looks like. A concrete success criterion helps the AI know when it has actually answered your question.
  • Iterate. Treat the first response as a draft — refine your request based on what came back rather than starting over.

These habits apply whether you’re asking a quick question or working through a longer, multi-step task.
