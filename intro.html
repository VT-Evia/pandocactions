Introduction

AI assistants can help with writing, research, coding, and countless other tasks, but they can only work with what you give them. Clear communication — stating your goal, sharing relevant context, and saying what “good” looks like — is the single biggest factor in getting a useful response. This guide covers practical habits for communicating with AI more effectively.
